# Sharing and Retention Policy

AI-assisted, directed by me

## 1. Where files live
All active business files (invoices, customer lists, price lists) live in the Shop files Shared Drive owned by the business. Personal My Drive is strictly for temporary working drafts only.

## 2. Sharing outside the business
Sharing outside the domain is turned OFF for the practice OU (or restricted to an allowlisted accountant domain with a warning). Default link sharing is set to Restricted, and existing public links must be closed by the file owner.

## 3. Who can sign in
Only active staff in the practice OU with 2-Step Verification enabled via authenticator app after the new-user enrolment period. Backup codes are printed on paper and stored physically—never saved digitally.

## 4. What we keep, how long, where
Business records (invoices, customer lists, price lists, financial records) are kept in the Shop files Shared Drive and monthly offline backups. Retention period: to be checked with the accountant.

## 5. The monthly export
Every month, business data (Drive, Mail, Calendar) is exported via Google Takeout as a .zip archive and saved offline to an external drive held by the owner.

## 6. Who holds admin
Two designated admins hold Super Admin / delegated access so the business is never locked out, each using 2-Step Verification with printed backup codes stored securely.

## 7. When someone leaves
Follow the 12-step offboarding checklist on the day they leave: export and transfer data, revoke sessions, remove account from mobile devices, and suspend the account—never delete it immediately.
