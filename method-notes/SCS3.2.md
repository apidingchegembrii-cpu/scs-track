# SCS3.2 Method Note

1. **What I set out to do:** Configure Drive sharing restrictions, 2-Step Verification enforcement, and basic device management on `/Trainees/renter`, draft and correct a one-page sharing and retention policy, offboard my Front Desk new starter using the 12-step checklist (suspend, never delete), verify four data-restore methods, and prepare the client backup drill.
2. **What I did, in order:** Created the evidence folder and repo files, started a Takeout export, tightened Drive sharing and moved 3 business files to `Shop files`, enabled 2-Step Verification with printed backup codes and device screen-lock rules, corrected the AI policy draft, ran the 8-loss inventory and 12-step offboarding with partner checks, tested all four restores, ran the timed rotation, left the OU whole, and exported the users and Drive logs.
3. **What went wrong, and how I fixed it:** The AI policy draft included Vault retention features missing from this Workspace edition and invented a 7-year retention period; I checked every setting against the live Admin console, struck unsupported features, and replaced the invented number with "to be checked with the accountant."
4. **How long it took, against the plan:** About 2 hours for the morning sharing, security, policy, and initial offboarding tasks, and 2 hours for the restores, rotation, client files, and final verification, matching the day's plan.
5. **One lesson for the next person:** Always write the change-log row before clicking in the console, start the leaver's Takeout export before suspending their account, and have the file owner manually restrict existing "Anyone with the link" files because default settings only apply to new files.
6. **What I might be wrong about, and how I would find out:** I might be wrong about whether my tier-1 client has hidden third-party accounts (supplier portals, registrar, or M-Pesa/WhatsApp channels) tied to an employee's phone or email, or what exact tax retention period applies to their records; I would find out by checking every login recovery email during the owner call and confirming the retention rule with their accountant.
