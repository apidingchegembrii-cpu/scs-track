# SCS2.3 Method Notes

1. I collected website content from the owner and recorded who provided each fact and when.
2. I only included information that the owner agreed to publish.
3. I avoided publishing private information, personal phone numbers, home addresses, or photos without consent.
4. I reviewed AI-drafted content and ensured I could explain every line used on the website.
5. I tested the website links and asked a partner to complete the five-question stranger test.
6. I reviewed the Definition of Done with my partner and identified any outstanding work for resubmission.
